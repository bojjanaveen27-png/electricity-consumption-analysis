Analytical Framework & Calculation Assumptions

  • Cumulative Unit: All meter readings represent raw cumulative Kilowatt-hours (kWh).
  • Interval Consumption: Consumption for interval \(i\) is calculated as \( \text{kWh}_i = \text{cumulative}_i - \text{cumulative}_{i-1} \).
  • First Reading Rule: A meter's first recorded reading sets the cumulative baseline and yields no prior consumption.
  • Meter Rollovers & Resets: If a subsequent reading is strictly lower than the preceding reading, the system marks the entry for RESET/ROLLOVER REVIEW and excludes it from automated consumption aggregation to prevent negative consumption distortion.
  • Chronological Period Grouping: Period grouping allocates consumption to the calendar month of the interval's ending date.
  • Threshold Alerts: Configurable threshold (currently {{ threshold }} kWh/month) detects surges and high-volume consumers.
